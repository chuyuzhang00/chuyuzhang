I am a multidisciplinary individual with a background in Mathematics and Management, currently pursuing my academic journey at Sungkyunkwan University. My interests lie at the intersection of technology, business, and human-centered innovation.

My academic journey began with a foundation in Mathematics at the University of Manchester, where I developed an analytical mindset and an appreciation for structured problem-solving. I later pursued a Master's degree in Management and Implementation of Development Projects, which broadened my understanding of organizational management, strategic planning, and the implementation of complex projects.

Beyond academia, I have gained experience in education, entrepreneurship, and international business. Through teaching English, developing business initiatives, and exploring cross-border e-commerce, I have developed a practical perspective on how ideas can be transformed into real-world solutions. These experiences have also strengthened my ability to work across different disciplines, cultures, and professional environments.

I am particularly interested in the evolving relationship between artificial intelligence, data analytics, and business. I enjoy exploring how emerging technologies can improve decision-making, create new business opportunities, and reshape the way organizations operate.

I believe that meaningful innovation happens when analytical thinking meets practical experience. My goal is to continue exploring this intersection through academic research, professional development, and real-world applications.
